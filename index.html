Scan with the phone camera

Reads the barcodes on bottles, cans and cases — EAN, UPC, Code 128, QR and more. The number pad opens the moment a scan lands, so counting is scan → type → next.

Stocktakes by location

Create a named stocktake per location — main bar, cellar, storeroom — and pause and resume any of them whenever you like, from any device.

Synced accounts

Every count is saved to the cloud as you go. Log in on another phone and pick up exactly where you left off.

Export anywhere

One tap exports the stocktake as CSV — download it, share it to email or WhatsApp, or paste it straight into your spreadsheet.

Running more than one venue?

Single-venue keeps one bar's counts in one place. Multi-venue does more — every location's stocktakes flow into the same organisation, so head office sees everything centralised, not scattered across each site's own spreadsheet. Export any stocktake, from any venue, in a format ready to import straight into your stock management system.

How it works

From signup to a finished stocktake in a few minutes.

1

Sign up

Create your account with a name, email and password.

2

Set up your organisation

Name it and add your first venue — or join an existing one with a code.

3

Choose a plan

Pick Single- or Multi-venue and check out securely with Stripe.

4

Scan

Point your phone's camera at a barcode — beep, done.

5

Count

The number pad pops up — type the quantity and save. Scan again to add to the count.

6

Export

Download or share the finished stocktake as CSV — ready for your books or stock system.
