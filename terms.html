1. What Gantry is

Gantry turns a phone into a barcode scanner for stocktaking. You scan products, enter quantities, and export the result as a CSV for your own books or stock system.

Gantry records the counts you enter. It does not check them, value them, or reconcile them against your POS. The numbers are yours and you are responsible for them — including checking an export before you act on it.

2. Accounts

You need an email address and a password. Keep the password to yourself: activity is attributed to the account that performed it, and that attribution is the whole basis of the activity log.

One person per login. Signing in on a new device signs the account out of the previous one. Accounts are not for sharing — if two people need to count, they need two logins.

An organisation has one owner, who controls billing, roles, and who can count. Owners and managers can create logins for their staff.

3. The trial

New organisations start on a free trial. No card is required and nothing is charged.

The trial allows five products per stocktake. You can create as many stocktakes as you like, each with its own five, and exports are not limited. Counting beyond five products in one stocktake requires a paid plan.

The trial has no time limit today. We may change or end it for new signups, but we will not remove data you have already counted.

Changing how many can count at once

You can increase it at any time and the change takes effect immediately. Each increase is billed for a minimum of one month, so you cannot add people for a busy weekend and remove them on the Monday. Adding more restarts that month. Decreases take effect once the minimum period has passed.

Billing country

Your billing country sets your currency. It can be changed once every 30 days. That limit exists to stop the country being switched to a cheaper currency immediately before checkout.

5. Billing

Payments are handled by Stripe. Subscriptions renew automatically — monthly or yearly, depending on what you chose — until cancelled. Yearly plans are charged in full up front.

If a payment fails, Stripe will retry. If it keeps failing the subscription ends and section 6 applies.

Manage your payment method, billing address and invoices yourself through the billing portal, reachable from Account in the app.

6. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time through the billing portal. There is no cancellation fee and no notice period.

Read this before you cancel. When a subscription ends, the organisation is locked: your data is kept, but you cannot open stocktakes or export them until you subscribe again. Export anything you still need before cancelling. Resubscribing restores access to the data as it was.

To remove the data as well, use Delete account in the app. That cancels the subscription immediately and deletes the organisation's stocktakes. It cannot be undone.

7. Refunds

We do not give refunds for part of a period you have already paid for, or for time you did not use the service. The trial exists so you can find out whether Gantry suits your venue before paying anything.

If something is genuinely broken, tell us — we would rather fix it or refund it than argue. Nothing in these terms excludes your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, which apply regardless of what this section says.

8. Acceptable use

Don't share logins, try to reach another organisation's data, attack or overload the service, or resell Gantry as your own product. We may suspend an account that does.

9. Availability

We aim to keep Gantry available and working, but we do not promise uninterrupted service. It depends on providers we do not control, and on your device and internet connection. Scanning needs a working camera and a connection to save.

We may change how features work. If a change materially reduces what you are paying for, we will tell you.

10. Your data

Your stocktake data is yours. We do not sell it or use it for anything other than providing the service. How it is handled is described in the Privacy Policy, which forms part of these terms.

Keep your own copies of anything that matters to you. CSV export exists precisely so your records do not depend on us continuing to exist.
