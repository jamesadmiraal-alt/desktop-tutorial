Venues

Staff can only start stocktakes at a location listed here. Locations are grouped under a venue.

Single-venue includes one venue, with unlimited locations inside it. Upgrade to Multi-venue from the app's Account page for unlimited venues.

Team

Staff scan and count: they create stocktakes, scan into them, and can remove individual scans to fix a mis-scan. They can't delete a whole stocktake or clear all its items.
Managers do all of that, plus manage venues and locations, delete stocktakes, clear items, and see this console — including who's logged in and the activity log.
Owner — you — additionally controls the team itself (adding people, changing roles, removing them) and everything billing: the plan, seats, region and codes. There's one owner.
Only the owner is free of a concurrent seat; managers and staff each use one while logged in.

Concurrent seats

Venues and locations are unlimited and free on Multi-venue — what's billed is how many people can be logged in at the same moment. You're always free; each extra seat is $29/mo.

Added seats are billed for a minimum of one month, so you can't reduce below a level you've just bought up to until that month is up. Adding more restarts the month.

How often devices check in, and how quickly a forced log-out or a crashed session actually frees its seat. Lower = faster, more requests.

Seats and billing are managed by the organisation's owner.

Activity log

Every deletion, quantity change, status change and team change, with who did it. Nobody can edit or remove an entry — not even you. Filters and sorting apply to the whole log, not just the page you're looking at.
